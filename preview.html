About Me

Ever since I got my first iPhone 4, I’ve been filming videos with friends and editing them in iMovie. What started as a hobby grew into a passion, and that passion has shaped my career path. I’m a creator fueled by ambition and drive, always looking for new ways to tell stories and connect with audiences.

I recently graduated from SUNY Cortland with a degree in Communications, where I built a foundation in media production and digital storytelling. Along the way, I grew @BarstoolSUNY from 76K to nearly 180K followers, interned at Barstool Sports working on shows like Healthy Debate and Only Stans, and produced and hosted my own podcasts from the ground up.

Whether I’m behind the camera, mixing audio in the booth, hosting on the mic, or performing on screen, I know how to take an idea from concept to creation and deliver content that resonates.

Looking ahead, my goal is simple: keep people entertained.
